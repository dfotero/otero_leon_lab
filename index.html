About the Lab

Our Mission

The Otero-Leon Lab at the University of Virginia develops analytical and computational tools to help healthcare systems make smarter, more equitable decisions. We combine optimization, simulation, and machine learning to address problems in disease prevention, clinical operations, and resource allocation — with the goal of improving outcomes for patients and providers alike.
